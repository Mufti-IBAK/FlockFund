# About Us

## Our Story

FlockFund was born from a simple observation: poultry farming is profitable, but the barrier to entry is high. Land, infrastructure, expertise, and capital — most people don't have all four. Meanwhile, those who *do* have capital are often looking for alternative investments that are tangible, ethical, and transparent.

We asked: *What if we could bridge the gap?*

Our mission is to democratise agricultural investment by allowing anyone to co‑own broiler chickens, earn profits, and be part of a growing community of forward‑thinking investors. No farm required. No expertise needed. Just a belief in something real.

## Our Mission

To make poultry farming accessible, transparent, and profitable for everyone.

# Terms of Service

**Last Updated:** March 2026

## 1. Acceptance of Terms

By using FlockFund, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and capable of entering binding contracts.

## 3. Account Registration

You are responsible for maintaining accurate account information and for all activity under your account.

## 4. Investment Services

### 4.1 Nature of Investment
Investing in FlockFund means you are co‑owning broiler chickens. You are **not** purchasing securities or shares. Returns are based on the profitable sale of birds.

### 4.2 Risk Acknowledgment
**You acknowledge that:**
- Poultry farming involves inherent risks including disease, mortality, and market fluctuations.
- You may lose part or all of your investment.
- FlockFund does not guarantee any return.

### 4.3 Profit Distribution
- Investor share: **70%** of flock profits
- FlockFund share: **30%** of flock profits

### 4.4 Reinvestment Rule
For the first **three completed cycles**, **20%** of your profit must be reinvested. After three cycles, you may withdraw any or all funds.

## 5. Reserve Fund

- **8% of FlockFund's profit share** from each flock is set aside in a Reserve Fund.
- The fund provides **partial compensation** in case of catastrophic losses (disease outbreak, government culling).
- It does **not** guarantee full recovery.
- Once the fund reaches **20% of total active investments**, excess is distributed as bonus birds to flock fund workers as incentives and bonuses for badged investors.

## 6. Farm Data

Anonymised farm data may be used for analytics and shared with third parties. Your personal information is never included.

## 7. Payment Terms

Payments are processed by third‑party gateways. Transaction fees apply. Investments are non‑refundable once a flock cycle begins.

## 8. Disclaimers

# Compliance Statement

**Last Updated:** March 2026

## 1. Our Commitment

FlockFund is committed to operating with integrity, transparency, and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. We believe that strong compliance protects both our investors and our business.

## 2. Investment Structure

Our model is structured as a **direct co‑ownership agreement**:

- Investors jointly own physical assets (broiler chickens).
- Profits are shared based on actual farm performance.
- This is distinct from securities or collective investment schemes.

## 3. Investor Protection

### Clear Risk Disclosure
All investors must acknowledge and accept the risks of agricultural investment before participating. Risk disclosures are prominently displayed.

### Segregated Funds
Investor funds are held separately from operational funds and used only for their intended purpose.

### Dispute Resolution
We maintain a fair dispute resolution process:
1. **Internal review** — complaint submitted to support
2. **Escalation** — reviewed by management
3. **Mediation** — if unresolved, referred to agreed mediator

## 4. Data Protection

We follow industry best practices for data protection, including:
- Collecting only necessary information
- Obtaining consent for processing
- Implementing reasonable security measures
- Honouring data subject requests

## 5. Anti‑Money Laundering

We take reasonable steps to prevent misuse of our platform, including identity verification for larger investments and monitoring for suspicious activity.

## 6. Tax Compliance

- Investors are responsible for reporting and paying taxes on their profits based on their individual circumstances.

## 7. Advertising & Marketing

Our marketing is honest and transparent. We do not:

- Target vulnerable populations

## 8. Risk Management

We maintain a comprehensive risk management framework including:
